# Content Strategy Inputs

Discovery can accept a brief from a future planning/orchestration skill: target audience, topic priorities, campaign objective, preferred duration, tone, repetition constraints, and previous editorial feedback. Treat it as ranking context, never as permission to bend source meaning.

Use feedback to adjust candidate selection transparently. Do not automatically change rubric weights; propose a rubric update with a new version and human approval. Metricool data and publishing operations belong to Phase 5 and are not part of this skill.

For an autonomous batch, prefer a useful mix of topics and videos after quality gates. For a requested topic, remain on topic instead of injecting unrelated variety.
